Upview Intelligence is the premier analytics suite for Roblox developers who need more than just a list of messages. It leverages state-of-the-art LLMs (including Llama 3.1 and Gemini) to synthesize your player data into high-level strategy.

## Key Features

### Deep Audit Reports
The Deep Audit is Upview's flagship intelligence feature. It analyzes up to 500 recent feedback entries across all categories and sentiments to produce a comprehensive "Game Health" report.

- **Automated Trend Detection**: Identifies growing dissatisfaction or praise for specific game mechanics.
- **Strategic Recommendations**: Provides actionable advice on what to fix or buff next based on player consensus.
- **Categorical Breakdown**: Visualizes the distribution of bugs vs. suggestions to help you allocate developer resources effectively.

### AI Data Chat
Imagine having a data analyst who has read every single piece of feedback your game has ever received. That's Upview Chat.

- **Context-Aware Responses**: Our AI pulls relevant context from your actual feedback submissions to answer your questions.
- **No More Data Silos**: Ask about specific forms, themes, or time periods without writing a single SQL query.
- **Refined by Hardware**: Uses a "Waterfall" engine that rotates through multiple high-performance AI providers to ensure 24/7 availability.

### Automated Thematic Clustering
Reading individual messages is slow. Upview's "Titration" engine uses AI to cluster similar feedback into "Themes".

- **Micro-Trends**: Spot that a specific sword is "too expensive" because the AI grouped 50 players saying different variations of the same thing.
- **Auto-Naming**: The AI generates human-readable titles for these clusters (e.g., *"Economic Imbalance"*), helping you navigate thousands of data points at a glance.

## How it Works (Under the Hood)
When feedback is submitted via the SDK, it's not just stored; it's **indexed**. We generate vector embeddings for every response, allowing our AI to "search" for similar feedback when you ask it questions. 

This hybrid approach—combining traditional categorization with semantic vector search—is what makes Upview Intelligence the most accurate data tool on the market.
