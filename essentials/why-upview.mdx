Upview isn't just a feedback tool; it's a dedicated intelligence engine for your game. We handle the heavy lifting of capturing, translating, and analyzing player data so you can focus on building a better experience.

## The Feedback Loop

At its core, Upview consists of three powerful layers that work together to turn raw player sentiment into actionable developer tasks.

### 1. High-Performance SDK
The Upview SDK is built specifically for Roblox, engineered for zero-latency impact on your game loop.
- **Context-Aware Analytics**: Every feedback submission automatically captures hardware specs, FPS, server memory, and player location. 
- **The Visual Builder**: Build and deploy complex feedback forms from your web dashboard in seconds—no game updates required.
- **Deep Lua Integration**: Use our declarative DSL to build custom, code-first UI that still benefits from our global backend.

### 2. Autonomous AI Classification (`ai-classifier`)
Once feedback hits our servers, it is immediately processed by our **AI Classification Pipeline**. This isn't just keyword matching—it's high-fidelity inference running on dedicated hardware.

- **Global Player Support**: Our translator automatically converts feedback from over 100 languages into English, ensuring you never miss a bug from your global audience.
- **Human-Centric Sentiment**: using optimized VADER analysis, we detect the emotional weight of every response. 
- **Urgency Scoring**: Upview understands the difference between *"The game is a bit laggy"* and *"THE SHOP IS COMPLETELY BROKEN"*. We calculate an urgency score (0-1.0) so your team handles critical issues first.
- **Auto-Categorization**: Feedback is instantly tagged into categories like `BUG`, `LAG`, `SUGGESTION`, `BALANCE`, or `TOXICITY`.

### 3. Upview Intelligence (`ai-intelligence`)
The final layer turns your dataset into high-level strategy. This is where your data becomes a competitive advantage.

- **Interactive AI Chat**: Talk to your game data. Ask questions like *"What are players saying about the new weapon?"* or *"Summarize today's lag issues."*
- **Thematic Clustering**: Our AI "titrates" your data, automatically grouping hundreds of similar responses into named trends (e.g., *"Shop Interaction Bug"*), so you see the big picture without reading every message.
- **Deep Audit Reports**: Generate professional-grade audits of your game's health, analyzing trends over weeks or months to help plan your next major update.
