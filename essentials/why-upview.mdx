---
title: "Why Upview?"
description: "What Upview is and why it exists."
---

Upview is an analytics platform for Roblox games. You install the SDK in 
your game, and data from your players starts appearing in your dashboard. 
But it's more than just fancy charts.

The main reason Upview was created is that most Roblox developers have no 
reliable way to understand what's happening in their game from the player's 
perspective. Discord feedback is hard to read at volume, Roblox's built-in 
analytics don't show you where or why things go wrong, and errors often go 
unreported entirely. Upview is the only tool built specifically to fix that, 
for Roblox, and nothing else.

## Features at a glance

**Feedback** — Players submit reports through forms you create in the 
dashboard. Each submission is automatically categorised, urgency scored, 
and grouped with similar reports. Instead of reading 200 Discord messages, 
you see "43 players reported a hitbox issue this week, urgency 8/10."

**Heatmaps** — Player positions, deaths, and emotion events are plotted 
directly onto your map. You upload a screenshot and align it once — from 
there, every event appears in the right location. If 80 players are 
rage-quitting in the same zone, you'll see exactly where.

**Error monitoring** — Errors are captured automatically and grouped by 
fingerprint so the same crash doesn't appear as 500 separate issues. You 
can generate an AI diagnosis that identifies the root cause and suggests 
a specific fix based on the actual stack trace — not just "something broke."

**Upview Intelligence** — Generate a Deep Audit that reads your data and 
tells you what to fix first, this week, and next update — in plain English. 
There's also a chat interface where you can ask questions like "why are 
players leaving after 4 minutes" and get answers based on your actual data.

## Who it's for

Upview is designed to save developers time. It's most useful for games 
with active players — if your game has no players yet, the dashboard will 
be empty until traffic comes in.

## Why it costs money

Running scalable and reliable infrastructure has real costs, especially 
the AI features. A 7-day free trial is available so you can see whether 
it's useful before subscribing. No credit card required.

## The SDK

The SDK is fully open source. You can read every line before adding it 
to your game.
